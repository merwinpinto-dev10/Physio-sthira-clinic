Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PHYSIOSTHIRA ("we", "us", "our") handles personal information when you use our website at www.physiosthira.in. We are committed to being transparent about the information we collect and how we use it.

By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, we recommend that you discontinue use of the website.

2. Who We Are

PHYSIOSTHIRA is a physiotherapy clinic located in Vidyaranyapura, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. We provide evidence-based physiotherapy services including pain management, sports physiotherapy, orthopaedic rehabilitation, post-surgical rehabilitation, neurological rehabilitation, geriatric physiotherapy, women's health physiotherapy, and home physiotherapy.

3. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us through the forms on our website. We do not use analytics tools, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies on this website.

3.1 Appointment Request Form

When you submit an appointment request through our booking form, you may provide:

  • Full Name (required)
  • Phone Number (required)
  • Email Address (optional)
  • Service you are interested in (selected from available options)
  • Visit Type — clinic visit or home visit (selected from available options)
  • Additional Information (optional — free-text field where you may describe symptoms, questions or other relevant details)

3.2 Contact / Enquiry Form

When you submit a contact enquiry through our enquiry form, you may provide:

  • Full Name (required)
  • Phone Number (required)
  • Email Address (optional)
  • Service you are enquiring about (selected from available options)
  • Message about your symptoms or condition (optional — free-text field)

3.3 Health and Symptom Information

Because PHYSIOSTHIRA is a physiotherapy clinic, some form fields allow you to optionally describe symptoms, conditions or health-related questions. This information is provided entirely at your discretion.

Please provide only the information you consider reasonably necessary for your enquiry or appointment request. The website forms are intended to facilitate initial contact and are not a substitute for a clinical consultation. Submitting information through the website does not in itself create a formal medical record or establish a patient-provider relationship.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information you provide through the website forms for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry or appointment request
  • To contact you by phone, email or WhatsApp to confirm or discuss your appointment
  • To understand the nature of your concern so that we can provide a more relevant response
  • To maintain appropriate business records
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your personal information for marketing, advertising, profiling or automated decision-making.

5. How Form Data Is Handled

When you submit an appointment request or contact enquiry, the information you provide is intended to be transmitted to our team so that we can respond to you. The website is designed to send your form submissions to a server for processing.

If you submit a form and see a confirmation message, this indicates that your submission has been processed on the website. If you do not receive a response from us within a reasonable time, we encourage you to contact us directly by phone or WhatsApp to confirm that your request was received.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We may use third-party service providers (such as hosting or communication services) to support the operation of our website or to process form submissions. Any such providers would process your information only on our behalf and in accordance with our instructions.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, regulation, legal process or enforceable government request.

7. Third-Party Services

This website uses certain third-party services. These services are operated independently, and we do not control how they process information. Each service is subject to its own privacy policy and terms.

7.1 Google Maps

We embed a Google Maps widget on our contact section to help visitors locate our clinic. This embed is loaded from Google and may be subject to Google's Privacy Policy. When you view the embedded map, your browser communicates directly with Google's servers, which may involve Google receiving information such as your IP address.

7.2 Google Fonts

This website loads fonts (Spectral and Hanken Grotesk) from Google Fonts. When a page is loaded, your browser makes a request to Google's font servers to retrieve the font files. This may result in Google receiving your IP address and information about the page being loaded. Google Fonts is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

7.3 WhatsApp

Our website includes links that open WhatsApp conversations with our clinic phone number. When you click a WhatsApp link, you are redirected to WhatsApp (operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.) and your interaction from that point is governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. We do not transmit any personal data to WhatsApp through these links — they simply pre-fill a greeting message for your convenience.

7.4 Google Reviews

Our website displays patient reviews. These are genuine reviews that have been manually selected from our Google business listing and are stored directly within the website's code. The website does not automatically fetch or import reviews from Google, and does not use the Google Places API.

A "View all the reviews on Google" button links visitors to our Google Maps listing. Clicking this link takes you to Google, which is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, or any similar browser storage technologies for tracking, analytics, or advertising purposes.

We do not use Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Facebook Pixel, or any other analytics or advertising tracking tools.

The third-party services embedded on this website (such as Google Maps and Google Fonts) may use their own cookies or similar technologies as part of their standard operation. These are set by the respective third-party providers and are governed by their own privacy policies.

9. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the personal information you provide through our website. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

We encourage you to exercise caution when sharing personal or sensitive health information online and to provide only the information you consider necessary for your enquiry.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries or appointment requests, maintaining appropriate business records, complying with applicable legal obligations, and resolving disputes.

11. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information, where applicable
  • Withdraw consent for the processing of your personal information, where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint or grievance regarding the handling of your personal information

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at physiosthira@gmail.com or call us at +91 91873 10374. We will respond to your request within a reasonable time.

12. Applicable Law

This Privacy Policy is written for an Indian business and is intended to be consistent with applicable Indian data-protection and privacy laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and any rules or provisions thereunder as and when they become applicable.

We will update this Privacy Policy as necessary to reflect changes in applicable legal requirements.

13. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian consent. If you believe that a child has submitted personal information through our website without appropriate consent, please contact us so that we can take appropriate steps.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, the website's functionality, or applicable legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
